Professional development insights

What effective professional learning makes possible

Use these research-informed principles as planning prompts, not a one-size-fits-all checklist.

01

Start with a meaningful, specific problem.

Ground learning in a priority people recognize in their practice, rather than a broad topic with no clear application.

02

Build in active practice and feedback.

Make space to rehearse, receive usable feedback, revise, and apply learning in the context where it matters.

03

Design for sustained support, not a single event.

Extend learning through coaching, collaborative inquiry, reflection, and follow-up that supports transfer into everyday work.

This studio draws on research syntheses and guidance from the Learning Policy Institute, Education Endowment Foundation, and teacher-coaching evidence. Sources are listed in the project README.

Choose a support rhythm that makes application visible and gives people a reason to return to the work.

A transparent tool

What this simulation does and does not claim.

It does: translate evidence-informed features of effective professional learning into a concrete planning conversation.

It does not: score institutional performance, validate a program, or predict outcomes. Your completion badge recognizes engagement with the studio, not a credential or certification.
